—Orders, closes, skipped copies and halts from the bot show here.
P/L is the price move only. eToro charges about 1% each way on crypto. 1x stocks are real shares with no overnight fee.
Prices are eToro's own live price, saved by the bot every minute; if none is fresh the paper account's price is used and labelled. From 2 Nov (US winter time) stocks trade 6:30 pm–1:00 am Dubai.
Total P/L = Closed P/L (eToro balance at cost minus the $10,000 funded, after fees) + Open P/L.
"Can eToro copy it?" shows the two strategy-level rules. Each trade must also pass its own checks before eToro copies it: stop no more than 8% below the entry, the trade planner, the setup's own backtest, no chase after a big run-up, and room in the account (9 positions, 3 copies a day, 5% daily loss stop). The bot's own gate decides; this table is a reading of the same data.
Retired strategies never trade again: etf mega cap relative strength rotation was retired on 1 Nov after 0 of 4 paper trades won (−$1,557) and all three losing eToro copies came from it.
Pre-market setups rank tradeable ones first: a full-strength signal from a strategy that passes the live evidence gate, on an allowed stock the bot doesn't already hold or have an order pending for. Then full-strength signals before weak ones, then by the backtest's expected R, then by reward to risk. At the open the bot's own checks and risk limits still decide every trade.
Versus holding SPY uses SPY's daily closes saved by the bot every 6 hours (from 1 Nov). The 1 Nov research found that the strategies' October winners came mostly from a rising market, so this comparison is the fairest test of whether the bot adds anything.
"Your copy" is AlgoBot's actual eToro P/L scaled to your $500 (500 / 10,000 = 5% of every trade). The bot cannot read your own eToro account, so eToro's copy rounding and fees make the app differ slightly: on 1 Nov the app showed $496.13 and this page $495.57.
